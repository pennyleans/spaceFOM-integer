Session control for the two-federate results.

The Pitch central component runs on the Windows host. After it has served
several runs, two failure modes appeared on a long-lived session: a single
same-tick update arriving after the observer's time advance grant, and in one
case no tick 1 updates at all. Restarting the Pitch application produced a
clean session on which both recordings pass byte-identically.

A/B on the long-lived session (before the restart), alternating the adapter
built from the pre-update sources and the current sources:

RESULT old1 publisher=1 observer=1 byte_identity=false err_pub='failure: timeout: MTR 3' err_obs='failure: time grant without complete frame'
RESULT new1 publisher=1 observer=1 byte_identity=false err_pub='failure: timeout: MTR 3' err_obs='failure: time grant without complete frame'
RESULT old2 publisher=0 observer=0 byte_identity=true
RESULT new2 publisher=1 observer=1 byte_identity=false err_pub='failure: timeout: MTR 3' err_obs='failure: time grant without complete frame'

Both adapters can fail on a long-lived session, so the failures are not
specific to the update-request change. The failed attempts are kept under
this directory; the passing runs were made after a Pitch restart.
