CRC layout
  The bundled Linux CRC cannot be used in this environment: the Free edition
  refuses headless mode ("pRTI Free edition does not support running in headless
  mode") and otherwise blocks on an interactive End User License Agreement
  dialog. The evidence for that attempt is in published-icrf-local-attempt1.
  The exchange therefore ran with our federates in WSL and the Pitch pRTI Free
  CRC in direct mode on the Windows host (same release, version 5.5.10 build 9905,
  CRC.port 8989), reachable from WSL at {crc-host}.

  The runner builds the adapter in a temporary directory against a shim whose
  headers are a copy of {work}/pRTI/api/cpp/HLA_1516-2010 and whose libraries
  link to lib/gcc73_64/librti1516e64.so and lib/gcc73_64/libfedtime1516e64.so.
  The Pitch headers already remove dynamic exception specifications under C++17
  through their RTI_THROW macro, so the copy is unmodified. The adapter sources
  were not modified.
